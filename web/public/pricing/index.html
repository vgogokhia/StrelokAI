Pricing

Ballistics.ge is free. Right now every feature, including Pro, is free for everyone.

Free — forever

  • Full ballistic calculator: trajectory, wind drift, spin drift, Coriolis
  • Automatic weather sync and GPS
  • Truing / velocity calibration without a chronograph
  • Reticle view and rangefinder
  • Works offline as an installed app (PWA)
  • 1 rifle and 3 loads stored on your device

Pro — USD 5, one-time (currently free for all users)

  • Unlimited rifle and ammunition profiles
  • Cloud sync between devices with Google sign-in
  • DOPE card export and printing
  • Truing history

Pro is free for now. If the number of users grows significantly, we may introduce a small one-time payment of USD 5 (no subscription, no renewals) to cover server and weather-data costs. We are not announcing a date.

Anyone who signs in before a fee is introduced keeps Pro free. Early users will never be asked to pay.

Questions

Payments, when introduced, will be processed by our merchant of record, Paddle.com, which handles taxes and refunds. See our Refund Policy and Terms of Service, or write to hello@ballistics.ge.
