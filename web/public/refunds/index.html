Refund Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Ballistics.ge is currently free, so there is nothing to refund today. This policy applies if and when a paid Pro upgrade is introduced.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you buy the one-time Pro upgrade and are not satisfied for any reason, request a refund within 14 days of purchase and you will receive a full refund. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

Email hello@ballistics.ge from the address linked to your account, or contact our merchant of record Paddle at paddle.net with your order number. Refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion, but we will always refund if Pro features did not work as described and we could not fix the problem.

Early users

Users who signed in before a fee was introduced keep Pro free and are never charged, so no refund situation arises.
