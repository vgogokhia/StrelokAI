Spin drift: why bullets drift right with no wind

Spin drift is the sideways movement of a spinning bullet, even in still air. A bullet from a right-hand twist barrel drifts right; left twist drifts left. For a .308 175 gr bullet at 1000 yards our solver predicts about 11 inches, around 0.32 MIL: small at short range, but worth correcting past about 600 yards.

Updated September 30, 2026 · ballistics.ge

What causes it

A spinning bullet's nose follows the curve of its trajectory, but it points very slightly to the side of its path (the "yaw of repose"). That small angle creates a sideways lift force in the direction of spin. The effect is tiny at first but grows with time of flight, so it matters most at long range where the bullet is falling steeply.

How much

Bryan Litz's empirical formula, used by most calculators including ours:

spin drift (in) = 1.25 × (SG + 1.2) × TOF1.83

where SG is the gyroscopic stability factor and TOF is the time of flight in seconds. For our .308 example (SG 1.90) that gives about 11 inches at 1000 yards. More stability, from a faster twist, means a little more spin drift.

Should you correct for it?

Inside 500 yards, spin drift is usually less than the rifle's group size, so most shooters ignore it. At 800–1000 yards it is similar to a 1–2 mph wind error, which is worth taking out. The calculator includes it in the wind correction automatically. Because it always goes the same way, some shooters simply move their zero slightly left for long-range rifles, but that makes the short-range zero wrong; letting the calculator handle it is better.

Separating spin drift from wind at the range

If you confirm long-range dope on a calm morning, you will notice your impacts are a little right of center with no wind. That is spin drift plus, in the northern hemisphere, a small Coriolis drift. Record it. When the calculator includes both effects and your group is still off to one side in calm air, check for reticle cant or a zero that is slightly right before blaming the model.

FAQ

Which way does spin drift go?

In the direction of rifling twist: right for right-hand twist barrels, which covers most rifles, and left for left-hand twist.

Is spin drift the same as Coriolis?

No. Spin drift comes from the bullet's rotation; Coriolis comes from the Earth's rotation. Both are small and both are handled by the calculator.

Does spin drift matter for hunting?

Rarely. At typical hunting distances it is under an inch or two.

Does spin drift change with distance in a straight line?

No, it grows faster than distance, because it depends on time of flight raised to the power 1.83. Doubling the time of flight more than triples the drift.
