.22 LR bullet drop and the best zero distance

A standard-velocity .22 LR (40 gr at 1070 fps) zeroed at 50 yards drops about 7 inches at 100 yards (1.9 MIL) and 53 inches at 200 yards (7.4 MIL). A 10 mph crosswind moves it about 4.2 inches at 100 yards. A 50-yard zero is the most common choice.

Why .22 LR drops so fast

The .22 LR bullet is light, short and blunt, with a G1 BC around 0.13–0.14, and it starts slow. It spends about 0.30 seconds reaching 100 yards, more than twice as long as a .308, so gravity and wind have more time to act. That makes rimfire an excellent, cheap way to learn wind reading: the drift you see at 100 yards with a .22 is similar to what a .308 shows at around 300–400 yards.

Which zero to choose

  • 50 yards: the most common choice. The bullet stays within about ½ inch of the aim point from 25 to about 60 yards, ideal for plinking and small game.
  • 25 yards: for indoor ranges and airgun-like distances; the bullet is then high at 50–75 yards.
  • 100 yards: for rimfire precision matches (NRL22, PRS rimfire) where you dial every distance anyway.

Subsonic and standard-velocity ammunition is usually more accurate than high-velocity, because high-velocity .22 LR at about 1250 fps crosses the speed of sound within the first 100 yards. See transonic.

Choosing .22 LR ammunition

  • Standard velocity match ammunition (SK, Lapua Center-X and Midas+, Eley Club and Tenex, RWS R50, CCI Standard Velocity) leaves the barrel at about 1050–1085 fps and gives the best accuracy.
  • High velocity (CCI Mini-Mag, Fiocchi Performance HV, SK High Velocity) is around 1235–1260 fps, flatter to 75 yards but it passes the speed of sound.
  • Subsonic (Fiocchi F320, CCI Subsonic, Eley Subsonic HP) runs about 1030–1050 fps and is quietest with a suppressor.
  • Hyper velocity (CCI Stinger, Velocitor) is for pests at short range, not for accuracy.

Every rimfire barrel has favorite ammunition. Test several brands at 50 yards before buying a case, and remember that the BC of .22 LR varies more between brands (0.12–0.17) than for centerfire bullets. Drop tables for each brand: all .22 LR loads.

FAQ

How far will a .22 LR shoot accurately?

Most shooters find 100 yards easy and 200 yards challenging. Rimfire matches go to 300 yards or more with good rifles, careful wind reading and match ammunition.

How much does a .22 drop at 100 yards?

With a 50-yard zero, about 7 inches (1.9 MIL / 6.6 MOA) for standard velocity. High-velocity ammunition drops a little less, around 4–5 inches.

What is the best zero for a .22 LR rifle?

50 yards for general use and small game. 100 yards if you shoot rimfire precision and dial for every distance.

Does a .22 LR need a special scope?

No, but a scope with parallax adjustment down to 25–50 yards helps, because many centerfire scopes are parallax-free only at 100 yards.

Is subsonic or high velocity .22 LR better?

For accuracy, subsonic and standard velocity. High velocity gives a flatter trajectory to 75 yards and more energy for small game.
