Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

We collect as little as we can. Most of what you enter never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Rifle and ammunition profiles, DOPE, settings and current conditions are stored in your browser (local storage). They are sent to us only if you sign in and enable cloud sync.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in (optional): your Google account ID, email address and display name, used only to identify your account and sync your profiles. We do not receive your password and do not store Google access or refresh tokens.
  • Synced profiles: if you sign in, your rifle/ammo profiles are stored on our server so other devices can load them.
  • Feedback: the text you send, an optional screenshot you attach, optional contact details, and technical context (app version, browser, screen size, selected profile) to help reproduce bugs.
  • Weather and location: when you use weather sync, your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch local conditions. If you are signed in, we also record the time of each weather sync and your approximate location rounded to about 1 km, so we can understand where and how often the app is used. We never store your exact position, we keep at most the last 500 entries per account, and this data is deleted with your account. If you are not signed in, nothing is recorded.
  • Server logs: standard technical logs (IP address, request time, user agent) kept briefly for security and debugging.
  • AI assistant (optional, signed-in users): when you use the assistant, your messages, any photos you attach (downsized; we do not store them), and a snapshot of your current calculator settings (rifle/ammo names and data, conditions, the current solution) are sent through our server to the AI provider we currently use — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT) or Google (Gemini) — to generate the reply. These providers process API data under their business terms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). Note that Google's free Gemini API tier may use submitted data to improve its products; we use paid tiers where available. We store only a daily count of assistant requests per account, not the conversation.
  • Payments (future): if a paid upgrade is introduced, checkout is handled by Paddle.com as merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we only receive confirmation that your account is upgraded.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your data, do not show ads, and do not use advertising trackers. If we add usage analytics, it will be aggregate and privacy-friendly, and this page will be updated.

Where data is stored

Our servers are hosted on Railway (United States) behind Cloudflare. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Synced data is kept while your account exists. Feedback is kept until resolved, then deleted. You can delete your synced data by signing out and removing your profiles, or by emailing us; we will delete your account and its data within 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or object to processing. Contact hello@ballistics.ge.

Children

The Service is intended for adults who lawfully own or use firearms. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
